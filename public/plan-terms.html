In plain words

  • A plan is a pack of sessions with one barber. You pay once. It does not renew by itself.
  • You can use a plan only for the services it lists, and only with the barber who sold it.
  • Each booking uses one session. The visit must start before the plan ends.
  • If you cancel in time, you get the session back. If the barber cannot serve you, you also get it back.
  • If you miss a visit, the session counts as used. You get one credit with the same barber.
  • Unused sessions end when the plan ends. We do not refund them.

These terms apply when you buy a plan from a barber on TrimSlot. They are part of the Terms of Service and should be read with the Refund, Cancellation and Credit Policy.

1. What a plan is

1.1 A plan is a prepaid pack of haircut sessions with one barber, for a set price, number of sessions, validity period and list of included services. It is not a recurring subscription: you pay once, there is no automatic renewal and no card is saved.

1.2 The barber (not TrimSlot) sets the plan's name, price, sessions, validity and included services, within limits that TrimSlot sets. Today the limits are: price ₦1,000 to ₦500,000; validity up to 90 days; up to 30 sessions. The limits can change; they never change a plan you have already bought.

1.3 The barber is the seller of the sessions and the provider of the service. TrimSlot runs the platform and collects the payment through Paystack.

2. Buying a plan

2.1 You see the full terms (price, sessions, validity in days, included services) before you pay. You pay online through Paystack. A plan is active only after Paystack confirms the payment to us (this can take a moment; use "check status" if needed). Until then you have nothing to use and nothing is reserved.

2.2 The terms you see are frozen at purchase. If the barber later edits or deletes the plan, your plan does not change.

2.3 If you start a plan checkout and do not pay, nothing is bought. Unpaid plan checkouts are removed after about 2 days (after we check with Paystack that no money was taken). If you pay twice for the same plan, the duplicate payment is refunded automatically.

2.4 Plans are not available while bookings are paused for maintenance, or if the platform has switched plans off.

3. Using sessions

3.1 When you book with the barber, choose "Use plan session". Each booking uses one session. You can use a plan only for the services it includes, and only with the barber who sold it.

3.2 The appointment must start before the plan ends. Plan sessions expire at the end of the validity period (the plan's end date and time, counted from the moment payment was confirmed). You cannot book an appointment that starts after the plan has expired.

3.3 If you cancel in time (at least 30 minutes before), the session is returned to the plan. If the barber cannot serve you, the session is returned.

3.4 If you miss a plan appointment and the barber marks you no-show, the session counts as used and you receive one session credit with the same barber (30 days, not cashable) as set out in the Refund, Cancellation and Credit Policy. That credit is worth the service price on the missed booking, which for a plan session can be more than that one session cost you under the plan.

3.5 Sessions are personal to you and cannot be transferred, shared, sold or exchanged for cash.

4. Expiry, refunds and cancellation of a plan

4.1 Unused sessions expire when the plan ends and are not refunded.

4.2 There is no change of mind period built into the app.

4.3 If you have paid and the plan did not activate, or you were charged twice, tell us — we will verify with Paystack and refund where due.

4.4 If the barber's shop is removed or suspended while you have unused sessions, [PROPOSED: we will try to arrange the sessions with another route, or refund the value of unused sessions on a pro-rata basis].

4.5 If the barber closes for a period (days off), the plan does not automatically extend.

5. Barber's obligations

The barber must honour every paid session until the plan expires, provide the included services with the same care as any customer, and not refuse a plan customer because they paid less. See the Barber Agreement.

6. Fees

TrimSlot's charge and the barber's share of the payment processing fee are taken from the barber's side of the plan price. When you buy a plan online you also pay the small booking fee shown at checkout, once, at purchase. No fee is charged again when you use a session.
