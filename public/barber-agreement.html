Effective date: 16 October 2026 · Version: 1.0.1

1. Independent business

You operate your own barbering business and are not an employee of TrimSlot. You are responsible for service quality, staff, hygiene, shop conditions, required permissions, taxes and compliance with applicable law.

2. Listings and availability

Provide accurate shop details, location, photos, services, prices and opening hours. Keep availability current, manage bookings through the app and update statuses truthfully. Do not list services you cannot provide or misrepresent your business.

3. Customers and personal information

Treat customers respectfully. Use customer information only to fulfil a booking or handle a legitimate support issue. Keep it confidential, do not misuse it for unrelated marketing and protect your account credentials. Report suspected unauthorised access or data incidents promptly.

4. Payments and plans

Prices and plan details must be accurate and shown to customers before purchase. Online payments and eligible refunds are handled through the provider shown in TrimSlot. Complete any payout setup required for online payouts. Honour valid bookings and prepaid sessions and follow the app's published cancellation, refund and credit policies.

Any platform charges and payout rules applicable to your account are shown in the app or account controls. Do not falsify payment or service status, manipulate reviews or evade applicable charges.

5. Content and conduct

Upload only content you have permission to use. Do not harass users, submit unlawful or misleading material, manipulate bookings or attempt unauthorised access. TrimSlot may remove content or restrict a listing for safety, suspected fraud, serious misuse or legal compliance.

6. Suspension and support

TrimSlot may pause or suspend a listing for serious or repeated breaches, safety concerns, suspected fraud or legal obligations. Where appropriate, we will explain the reason and how to raise a concern. Use the Help option for booking, account or payout issues.

7. Updates

The current version and effective date appear above. These interim terms do not remove mandatory rights or obligations under applicable law.
